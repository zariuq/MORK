KEY OPTIMIZATION: Bidirectional Pattern Matching
-------------------------------------------------
Instead of separate S and P tables:

  OLD WAY (redundant):
    S(4, 5)   - successor: 4 → 5
    P(5, 4)   - predecessor: 5 → 4

  NEW WAY (optimized):
    S(4, 5)   - BOTH directions!
    
  Pattern matching works bidirectionally:
    S(4, $s)  matches S(4, 5) → $s = 5  (successor)
    S($p, 5)  matches S(4, 5) → $p = 4  (predecessor)

FIBONACCI RULE (Generic):

    (, (fib $n $id)
